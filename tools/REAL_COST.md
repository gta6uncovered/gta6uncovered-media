# GTA 6: The Real Cost — series rate book and episode log

The series: Mohamed (a Quantity Surveyor in Dubai) prices GTA 6 places with real construction and property costs.

Rules for every episode:
- Show the method on screen as QUANTITY x RATE = COST.
- Cite every rate (source and year) in SOURCE and in the description.
- Show every assumption on screen and label it "ASSUMPTION".
- Put the ESTIMATE tag on every cost card.
- Hard cost ≠ market price. Show land separately.
