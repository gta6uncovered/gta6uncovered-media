THE METHOD (estimate, late-2025 prices, hard cost + 20% fees)
- Homes: 1,104,711 Miami-Dade homes x $302K average rebuild cost = $333.6B (cross-check: 1.33B sq ft x $240/sq ft = $318B)
- Shops, offices, warehouses: 2025 appraised value used as rebuild cost = $133.6B (includes land, so on the high side)
- Roads: 5,500 miles x $11.09M per mile (FDOT urban arterial) = $61.0B
- Airport, port, stadium, bridge (minimum values): $15.7B
- Subtotal $543.9B + 20% design, fees and management = $652.7B
Assumptions: Vice City is treated as Miami-Dade County. Average home 1,200 sq ft. All county roads at arterial rate. 20% fees. Land is shown separately. Excluded: water/sewer, power, schools, hospitals, government buildings, parks, canals, expressways.
