# GTA6 Uncovered: lessons (rewritten daily by the learning task)

Evidence so far: only 3 old-style YouTube Shorts are live (001-003). New-style 004-010 go live 24-27 Nov.
Treat "evidence" lines as weak until we have 7+ new-style videos with retention data.

## Doing more of
- Contradiction/comparison hooks in the title: "Critics gave GTA 6 a 99. Players gave it a 5.6" got 1,213 views and 9 likes
  (best like rate, 0.74%) vs "GTA 6 in 6 numbers" 249 views, 1 like. Specific numbers that clash beat a vague list.
- Slot is NOT yet proven: 003 (7 PM UAE) 1,213 and regions (10:45 AM UAE) 1,292 did similar; only the 1 AM UAE post (249) lagged. Keep Slot A / B and measure.
- Hook with a number or curiosity gap in the first 1-2 s; the first words are the payoff tease.
- Real trailer footage, a cut every ~1.2 s, a sound for every visual change, words popping in captions.
- Snowball: fact -> "then" -> "but then" -> big number -> question. End on a debate question cut hard so it loops.
- Sources on screen; fresh news first (decays in 2-3 days).

## Stop doing
- Map/infographic-only visuals with no real footage (001-003; owner found them "not attractive").
- 26-34 s length. 001-003 ran 26-34 s; new batch is 18-21 s. Stay at 15-21 s.
- Generic "list" titles ("in 6 numbers", "in 30 seconds") with no tension: lowest views and likes.
- Zero comments on all 3 live videos: the ending question was weak or missing. Every video must end on a this-or-that question.
- Comparisons that mix different measures (e.g. YouTube-only vs all-platform views).
