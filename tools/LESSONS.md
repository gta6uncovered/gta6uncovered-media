# GTA6 Uncovered: lessons (rewritten daily by the learning task)

## Doing more of (starting rules from research, not yet tested on our own numbers)
- Hook with a number or curiosity gap in the first 1-2 s; the first words must be the payoff tease.
- Real trailer footage, a cut every ~1.2 s, a sound for every visual change, words popping in captions.
- Snowball structure: fact -> "then" -> "but then" (music drops out) -> big number -> question.
- End on a debate question cut hard so the video loops (comments + rewatches).
- Sources shown on screen; fresh news first (decays in 2-3 days).

## Stop doing
- Map/infographic-only visuals with no real footage (videos 001-003 felt "not attractive" to the owner).
- Comparisons that mix different measures (e.g. YouTube-only vs all-platform views).
